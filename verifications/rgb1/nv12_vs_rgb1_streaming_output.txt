'batch adv.' is how much larger the per-frame total is than the batch
figure -- i.e. the cross-frame dedup a real-time sender cannot use.
Measured at 1920x1080 over 8 frames it is at most 0.34%, and the 'none'
row shows ~0.04% of that is just per-frame tar headers, not compression.
The reason is window size: one frame is 3.1 MB (nv12) or 6.2 MB (rgb24),
larger than zstd/gzip/lz4 can look back across, so they cannot reference
the previous frame at all. xz, with the largest dictionary, shows the
biggest gap (+0.34% on rgb24) -- which is still negligible.
Conclusion: batching 8 frames does NOT inflate the per-frame bitrate here,
so the batch figures are safe to quote. Re-check this if the resolution
drops far enough for a frame to fit inside a codec's window.
