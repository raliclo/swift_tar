[Retired] 2026-08-15 — this record was produced from a synthetic corpus and
cannot be reproduced by the current script.

test_swift_tar_rgb1.sh built its codec table from a 4 KiB /dev/urandom block
repeated 768 times. That data is perfectly periodic, so every codec with a
window of 4 KiB or more matched immediately and the table reported zstd at a
ratio of 0.001 — where the same script on a real 1080p frame reports 0.443.
The block was also re-randomised on every run, so the numbers were never
comparable across runs or platforms; the 7-byte difference between the mac and
Windows records, once read as a library difference, was only a different corpus.

The script now requires a sampled video frame and skips the table when none is
present, so these figures can never be regenerated in this form. They are
removed rather than kept with a warning: the ratios are wrong by a factor of
450 for the thing the rows name, and a reader who skims a table does not read
its header.

To restore this record on Windows:
  1. verifications/rgb1/make_consecutive_corpus.zsh   (needs the source video)
  2. ./test_swift_tar_rgb1.sh
